Lajares guide

Things to do in Lajares and the north of Fuerteventura

Lajares is a small village between volcanoes and the Atlantic, loved by surfers, artists and families. These are the places we always recommend to our guests.

01

Calderón Hondo volcano

One of the island's best short hikes starts right by the village. Follow the trail through the lava fields up to the rim of the crater and look straight down into it. Watch out for the friendly Barbary ground squirrels.

Tip: Go early or at sunset, and take water and a windbreaker.

02

El Cotillo

An old fishing village 10 minutes away with two faces: to the north, the calm turquoise lagoons, perfect for children and snorkelling; to the south, long wild beaches under the cliffs. Fresh fish restaurants by the harbour.

Tip: The lagoons are best at low tide.

03

Surf in the north

The north shore is one of Europe's surf hotspots. Experienced surfers head to the reef breaks along the coastal track towards Majanicho; beginners learn on the beaches of El Cotillo and Corralejo with local surf schools.

Tip: Ask us which spot works with the day's wind and swell.

04

Lajares Saturday market

Every Saturday morning the village fills with craft stalls, local cheese, music and street food. A relaxed way to start the weekend and take a piece of the island home.

Tip: Try queso majorero, the island's famous goat cheese.

05

Corralejo dunes

About 15 minutes away, the Corralejo Natural Park is a sea of white sand dunes meeting turquoise water. Long beaches for walking, swimming and kitesurfing.

Tip: Bring shade: there are few trees in the dunes.

06

Lobos island

A short ferry ride from Corralejo takes you to Lobos, a tiny protected island with a lagoon-like beach and a walking trail around its volcano.

Tip: Visits need a free permit booked online in advance.
