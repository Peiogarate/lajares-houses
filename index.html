The village

Lajares, the soul of the north

Between the Calderón Hondo volcano and the Atlantic, Lajares is where surfers, artists and families come to slow down. Saturday craft market, small cafés, surf schools and dirt tracks to the lava fields, all within walking or cycling distance.

Questions, answered

Why book direct instead of a booking platform?

You talk straight to the owners, get a personal welcome and avoid platform service fees. The calendar on this page is synced with our bookings, so the dates you see are real.

Is the pool really heated all year?

Yes. Mali House and Dudi House each have a private heated pool, so you can swim comfortably in winter too.

Do I need a car?

In Lajares you can walk to restaurants, cafés and shops. To explore the beaches of El Cotillo, Corralejo and the rest of the island, a car or bike makes it easy.

How does the booking request work?

Pick your dates in the calendar and send the request. We reply within 24 hours with the total price and the steps to confirm. Nothing is charged until you agree.
